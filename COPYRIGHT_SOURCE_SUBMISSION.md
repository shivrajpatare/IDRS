## 4. PROJECT OVERVIEW & ARCHITECTURAL NOTES

### Workflow-Oriented Disaster Coordination
IDRS treats disaster response as a dynamic workflow rather than a static map view. It manages the complete lifecycle of a disaster—from the PRE-DISASTER warning phase to the MID-DISASTER tactical intervention and the POST-DISASTER recovery synchronization.

### Operational Synchronization (OpSync)
The core intellectual property of IDRS lies in its ability to synchronize disparate data streams (Sachet alert feeds, GPS citizen telemetry, and Responder asset status) into a unified Command Coordination Dashboard. This ensures that every tactical recommendation is grounded in real-time operational truth.

### AI-Assisted Backend Orchestration
The backend leverages neural inference to evaluate the threat level of incoming distress signals, providing commanders with a "Confidence Score" and actionable tactical plans generated by the IDRS Tactical AI advisor.
